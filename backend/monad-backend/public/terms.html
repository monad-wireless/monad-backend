Quests

A quest may ask you to:

  • walk to a place in the study building and follow the instructions on the screen;
  • scan printed QR markers with the camera;
  • keep the App open while the phone broadcasts a Bluetooth identity frame;
  • count the people you can see and enter the number.

Follow building rules and pay attention to your surroundings while you walk.

Device permissions
  • Bluetooth: to broadcast the identity frame and to find nearby Bluetooth devices during a quest.
  • Camera: to read QR markers and, during a measurement walk, to track the phone's own movement. No photos or video are saved.
  • Notifications: study messages through Firebase Cloud Messaging.

The App does not request location. You may refuse a permission, but some quests will not work without it.
