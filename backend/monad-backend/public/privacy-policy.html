Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the Monad Scan app ("the App") for iOS and Android. The App is a research instrument for a doctoral study at the Faculty of Informatics and Information Technologies, Slovak University of Technology in Bratislava (FIIT STU). The study estimates how many people are in a room from Wi-Fi and Bluetooth signals. The researcher responsible for the App and for this policy is Jakub Dubec, FIIT STU, jakub.dubec@stuba.sk ("we").

Who the App is for

The App is for university students and staff who volunteer for the study. Access is by invitation. Taking part is voluntary, and you may stop at any time. The App is not directed at children.

Account data
  • Email address.
  • Name, if you give one.
  • Password, stored on our server only as a one-way hash.
  • Account status (for example, waiting for approval or active), and your notification preferences.

We use the account to sign you in, to approve invited participants, to link your measurements to a pseudonym, and to send you study messages.

Measurement data from quests

When you take part in a quest, the App records and uploads:

  • The text of each QR marker you scan, with the time.
  • Step start and completion times, and skipped steps with the reason you give.
  • Counts of people that you enter yourself, when a step asks for one.
  • Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) records: the identity frame your phone broadcasts during a quest, and the advertisements of nearby BLE devices that a quest step asks the App to find (device address, name, signal strength, advertised services and manufacturer data, with times).
  • Check-in and check-out scans for room counts. These carry an opaque participant pseudonym, never your email or name.
  • A description of your phone: an installation identifier that the App creates (a reinstall makes a new one), the platform, manufacturer, model, chip, operating system version and build, app version, and the phone's radio and sensor capabilities. We do not collect the platform's advertising or vendor identifiers.

During a measurement walk, the App also records the phone's own movement through the room (its position and orientation relative to where the walk started) and, on phones with LiDAR, a 3D outline of the room's surfaces. The camera is used to compute that movement. No photos or video are saved or uploaded.

What the App does not collect
  • Location. The App does not request or read your location on iOS or Android.
  • Photos, video or audio.
  • Advertising identifiers. The App contains no advertising.
Instrument health readings

While a quest records, the App sends health readings about the measurement itself (for example the Bluetooth broadcast rate, recording state, clock offset and walk tracking quality) to our own monitoring server. Each reading carries only the site, the platform and your participant pseudonym.

Crash reports

The App uses Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC) to report crashes. Crash reporting is on. The App does not yet offer a switch to turn it off. A crash report contains the device model, operating system version, app version and build, the stack trace and the app state at the moment of the crash. It also carries your participant pseudonym and the site, so that we can match a crash to a measurement session. It does not contain your email or name. See Firebase privacy information.

Push notifications

The App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google LLC) to send study messages. If you allow notifications, our server stores a push token for your device. Quest call-outs are off until you turn them on in the App. You can turn notifications off in the App or in your phone's settings.

Where the data is stored

Account data and the measurement register are stored in a database on our server at api.monad.dubec.dev, hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH. Uploaded measurement files are stored in Hetzner Object Storage. Crash reports and push delivery are processed by Google LLC as described above.

Who can see the data

Only the researcher and the administrators of the study's server can see account data. We do not sell data or share it for advertising. Published results use aggregated or pseudonymous data only. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

Legal basis

We process your data on the basis of your consent to take part in the study (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You give it when you register and when you start a quest, and you can withdraw it at any time.

Retention

We keep account data while your account exists. Pseudonymous measurement data is kept for the research study. [RETENTION PERIOD: to be set by the researcher before publication.]

Deleting your data and your rights

You can delete your account in the App, under My Account. Deletion removes your email, name and password from our server at once. Your pseudonymous measurements stay, because they no longer identify you and the study depends on their counts. You have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. To use these rights, write to jakub.dubec@stuba.sk. You may also complain to the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov Slovenskej republiky).

Security

All traffic between the App and our server uses HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as hashes. The management interface is limited to administrator accounts.

Changes

We will publish any change to this policy on this page and tell participants in the App or by email.
